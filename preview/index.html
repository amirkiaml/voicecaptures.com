Frequently
asked questions

Does it sound like a robot?

Call the demo line and judge for yourself — that is the whole reason the number is on the site. It is not a phone tree and it does not read a script.

Does it use my existing number?

Yes. You forward your line to it; the caller sees and dials the number they already have. Nothing about your listings or signage changes.

What happens when it cannot answer something?

It takes the caller's details, tells them you will call back, and texts you the summary. For anything it is set to treat as urgent it transfers to you live.

How long does setup take?

About ten minutes of your time. We configure the assistant around your services, booking rules and calendar — you do not build anything.

What do you keep, and for how long?

Call history, recordings and transcripts are kept 14 days on every plan. Longer history is an add-on. For clinics we recommend staying at 14 days.
