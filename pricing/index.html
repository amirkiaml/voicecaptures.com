      Minutes, add-ons and setup

      If you need more minutes

      • We text you at 80% of your minutes, and again at 100%.
      • Your line never stops answering. We do not cut you off for going over.
      • Top up from your dashboard: 50 minutes for $65 ($1.30 each) or 100 for $120 ($1.20 each).
      • Minutes used past your plan without a top-up are $1.50 each, on your next invoice.
      • Top-ups apply to the month you buy them in. Unused minutes do not carry over.

      Consistently over? A larger plan is cheaper than topping up every month — we will tell you when that is true for you.

      Add-ons

      • Extra line or calendar — $29/mo
      • 90-day call history — $19/mo
      • 1-year call history — $49/mo
      • Practice-software, field-service, POS or CRM integration — from $500 one-time + $39/mo, quoted

      Every plan keeps call history, recordings and transcripts for 14 days. For clinics we recommend staying at 14 days — on a patient-facing line, keeping less is the safer setting, not a worse one.

      Setup

      • Setup is included. We configure the assistant for your business, on your existing number. It takes about 10 minutes of your time.
      • Multi-location or custom call flows — $249 one-time.

      What a minute means

      • Time our assistant spends on a live call, including transfers.
      • Calls the assistant marks as spam are not billed.
