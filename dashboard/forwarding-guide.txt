Send the calls you don't answer to your receptionist at {number}. Choose how you'd like to set it up:

OPTION 1: PHONE SETTINGS

Android (Samsung, Pixel)
1. Open the Phone app, tap the ⋮ menu, then Settings.
2. Find Call forwarding. On a Samsung it is under Supplementary services; on a Pixel it is under Calls or Calling accounts. Choose Voice call if asked.
3. Turn on three of them and set each to {number}: When unanswered, When busy, and When unreachable.
4. Leave Always forward off, or your phone will never ring.
5. If it lets you choose a ring time for When unanswered, pick 15 seconds.
Menu names differ between models and carriers. If you can't find them, use Dial a code instead.

iPhone
1. Don't use Settings > Phone > Call Forwarding for this. On iPhone it forwards every call, all the time, so your own phone would never ring.
2. Use Dial a code instead. It forwards only the calls you don't answer.

OPTION 2: DIAL A CODE
1. Open the keypad in your Phone app on the mobile whose calls you want forwarded.
2. Type the code exactly as shown, then press Call.
3. Wait for the confirmation message on your screen.

Forward the calls you don't answer (recommended)
Your phone rings for 15 seconds. If you don't answer, your receptionist picks up before voicemail does.
Turn on:  **61*{number}**15#
Turn off: ##61#

Forward when busy, unanswered or unreachable, all at once
One code for all three. It uses your carrier's own ring time instead of 15 seconds.
Turn on:  **004*{number}#
Turn off: ##004#

To check: dial *#61#. It should show {number} for unanswered calls.
To turn it off: dial ##61#, or ##004# if you used that code.

TURN OFF YOUR CARRIER'S VOICEMAIL
If your carrier's voicemail answers first, the call never reaches your receptionist, even when your phone says forwarding is set.
- Koodo: Tested on an iPhone: the forwarding code showed "Setting Registration Succeeded … When Unanswered", but Koodo voicemail still answered first. Turning voicemail off fixed it. Ask Koodo to turn voicemail off on your line.
- Other carriers (unverified): Bell, Rogers, Telus, Fido, Freedom Mobile, Virgin Plus, Public Mobile. We haven't tested these yet. Ask your carrier to turn voicemail off on your line.

TEST IT
1. From another phone, call your own number.
2. Let it ring. Don't decline it on your phone.
3. After about 15 seconds your receptionist should answer.
We can't see from here whether your forwarding is on, so this test is the way to check.

QUESTIONS

It still goes to voicemail
Your carrier's voicemail is answering before the forward. Turn voicemail off, then test again.
A success message from the code doesn't mean voicemail is off. On Koodo, the code reported success and voicemail still answered. The code ##002# also reported success and didn't change voicemail.

Do I have to turn voicemail off?
Usually, yes. If voicemail picks up first, your receptionist never gets the call. With voicemail off, a call you don't answer goes to your receptionist, who takes the message instead.

How do I test it?
Call your own number from another phone and let it ring. Don't decline it: declining sends the call to voicemail or your carrier's busy handling, so it isn't a fair test.
After about 15 seconds your receptionist should answer.

How do I turn it off?
Dial ##61#, and ##004# if you used that code. If you used your phone's settings, switch those back off.
Turn your voicemail back on if you want it.

Which carrier?
These codes are standard, but each carrier handles them its own way. Some need call forwarding added to your plan before any code works, and some charge for forwarded minutes.
We've tested Koodo. We haven't tested the others yet. If something doesn't work, ask your carrier to turn on conditional call forwarding (the name they'll know it by), or contact us.

Not on a mobile?
The codes above are for mobile phones. They will not work on a landline.
A landline is forwarded by the company that provides the line, not from the handset. Ring them and ask for "call forward on no answer" to {number}. Most providers have their own dial code for it, so ask them for it.
An office phone system or internet line (RingCentral, Vonage, 8x8, Google Voice, or a PBX) is set in your provider's admin portal or app. Look for Call Forwarding, Call Handling or Answering Rules, and forward calls that are not answered to {number}, after the last phone in any ring group.
