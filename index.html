Will it sound robotic?

It runs on current conversational voice models, and most callers don't register that it isn't a person. Call the live demo line and judge it for yourself before you commit to anything.

What if I already have a receptionist?

It takes overflow and after-hours rather than replacing anyone. Your team keeps the calls they can reach, and nothing rings out when they can't.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live in about ten minutes. You forward your line; we configure the greeting, hours, slot length and calendar.

Can I keep my number?

Yes — your published number never changes. You forward it to us, and callers only ever see the number they already had.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, it's month to month with no contract. Cancel and forwarding reverts to your own phone the same day.

What happens after the free trial?

You pick a tier and billing starts, or you do nothing and the line simply stops answering. We don't take a card up front, so nothing charges automatically.

Can I use it for after-hours only?

Yes — that's exactly what the entry tier is. Forward evenings, weekends and holidays, and leave daytime calls ringing through to you.

What about my sector?

Home services, restaurants and clinics are where we work today. The mechanics are the same anywhere the phone is how work arrives — someone calls, needs qualifying, and needs a slot booked. If yours is close, start the free trial and we'll tell you honestly whether it fits before you pay anything.
