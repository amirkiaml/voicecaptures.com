      Does it sound like a robot?

      Call the demo line and judge for yourself — that is the whole reason the number is on the site. It is not a phone tree and it does not read a script.

      Does it use my existing number?

      Yes. You forward your line to it; the caller sees and dials the number they already have. Nothing about your listings or signage changes.

      What happens when it cannot answer something?

      It takes the caller's details, tells them you will call back, and texts you the summary. For anything it is set to treat as urgent it transfers to you live.

      How long does setup take?

      About ten minutes of your time. We configure the assistant around your services, booking rules and calendar — you do not build anything.

      What do you keep, and for how long?

      Call history, recordings and transcripts are kept 14 days on every plan. Longer history is an add-on. For clinics we recommend staying at 14 days.

      Will it sound robotic?

      It runs on current conversational voice models, and most callers don't register that it isn't a person. Call the live demo line and judge it for yourself before you commit to anything.

      What if I already have a receptionist?

      It takes overflow and after-hours rather than replacing anyone. Your team keeps the calls they can reach, and nothing rings out when they can't.

      How long does setup take?

      Most businesses are live in about ten minutes. You forward your line; we configure the greeting, hours, slot length and calendar.

      Do I keep my number?

      Yes. Your calls forward to us when you don't pick up, or after hours. Nothing changes for your customers.

      Can I cancel anytime?

      Yes, it's month to month with no contract. Cancel and forwarding reverts to your own phone the same day.

      What happens after the trial?

      Your plan starts on day 15. We email you a reminder before then. Cancel before day 15 and you pay nothing.

      What counts as a minute?

      Time the assistant spends on a live call, including transfers.

      What if I go over?

      We text you at 80%. If you go over, you can add 100 minutes for $100, or move up a plan. You won't get surprise charges.

      I have more than one location. How does pricing work?

      Each plan covers one location. For two or more, book a 30-minute call and we'll quote one price for all of them, on one account and one bill.

      Where is my call data stored?

      The records we keep — recordings, transcripts and call details — are stored in Canada. The live call runs on providers in the United States: our phone provider stores no call audio, and our AI provider holds a copy of the conversation for 14 days before deleting it, which can take up to a further 30 days to clear its backups. Our Privacy Policy sets out the detail.

      How do you handle patient information?

      Our assistant collects only contact details and the general reason for the call. Recording is off by default for clinics, and transcripts are deleted after 14 days unless you choose longer. We'll work with your clinic on any privacy agreement your practice requires.

      Is win-back cold calling?

      No. We only contact your own past customers, people who have already bought from you, and every message identifies your business and lets them opt out.

      Can I use it for after-hours only?

      Yes — that's exactly what the entry tier is. Forward evenings, weekends and holidays, and leave daytime calls ringing through to you.

      What about my sector?

      Home services, restaurants and clinics are where we work today. The mechanics are the same anywhere the phone is how work arrives — someone calls, needs qualifying, and needs a slot booked. If yours is close, start the free trial and we'll tell you honestly whether it fits before you pay anything.
